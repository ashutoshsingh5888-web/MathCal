Why speed math, specifically

In SSC CGL Tier 1, you get roughly 45–50 seconds per quant question. In RRB NTPC, it's tighter still. Bank PO prelims quant sections are notorious for exactly this bottleneck — most aspirants don't lose marks because they don't know the method, they lose time recalculating multiplication tables and squares that should be instant recall.

That's the specific gap this app targets: tables, squares, cubes, and the calculation shortcuts that turn a 20-second manual multiplication into a 3-second lookup.

What's actually in here

  • Daily Test — a fixed mix across every topic, drives a day-streak so practice stays consistent.
  • Learn mode — full reference tables (10–30 multiplication, squares 1–40, cubes 1–20) with self-check recall, not just a static chart.
  • Practice mode — timed, difficulty-adjustable drills per topic.
  • Automatic review — anything you get wrong gets queued until you can answer it correctly twice in a row.
  • Shortcut guide — the actual tricks (×11, base-100 multiplication, squaring numbers ending in 5, and more) with worked examples.
