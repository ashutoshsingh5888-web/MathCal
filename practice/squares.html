Squares practice

This drill generates squaring questions (n²) across easy, medium and hard ranges, so you're not just memorizing a fixed list — the numbers change every session while the underlying patterns stay the same.

Certain ranges have genuine shortcuts worth learning rather than calculating from scratch: numbers ending in 5, numbers close to 50, and numbers close to 100 each have a fast mental method. All three are broken down step by step in the mental math shortcuts guide.
