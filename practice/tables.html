Multiplication tables practice

This drill covers multiplication tables from 2 up to 20, adjustable by difficulty. Easy sticks to the tables most people learn first (2–10); medium and hard extend further and mix in larger factors so recall stays sharp instead of settling into memorized patterns for just a few tables.

The fastest way through the higher tables (11–20) is usually not brute memorization — the shortcuts guide covers the ×11, ×9 and ×99 tricks, along with the "same tens, complementary units" method, all of which apply directly here.
