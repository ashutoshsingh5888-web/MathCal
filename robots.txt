      <h1>About Mental Math Daily</h1>
      <p>
        Mental Math Daily is a free practice tool for the calculations
        that show up constantly in exams, interviews, and everyday
        estimation: multiplication tables, squares, cubes, powers and
        general arithmetic.
      </p>
      <p>
        The design goal is short, frequent, honest practice rather than
        long occasional sessions. Every answer is scored immediately,
        accuracy is tracked per topic (not just overall), and anything
        answered incorrectly is automatically queued into a review list —
        it stays there until you answer it correctly twice in a row,
        rather than disappearing after one lucky guess.
      </p>
      <p>
        A daily test mode pulls a fixed mix of questions across every
        topic and drives a day-streak counter, as a light nudge toward
        practicing regularly instead of cramming.
      </p>
      <h2>How your data is handled</h2>
      <p>
        All progress — accuracy, streaks, session history, and the review
        queue — is stored locally in your browser's storage. Nothing is
        sent to a server, there's no account or sign-up, and clearing
        your browser data or using a different device starts fresh.
      </p>
      <h2>Offline &amp; installable</h2>
      <p>
        The app is a installable Progressive Web App — it can be added
        to your home screen or desktop and used fully offline once
        installed, so practice isn't dependent on a working connection.
